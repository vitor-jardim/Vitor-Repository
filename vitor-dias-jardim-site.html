About

I'm a medical student at Faculdade Ciências Médicas de Minas Gerais (FCM-MG). Brazil's public health system caps most consultations at around 15 minutes — so I set out to see how much of that time was being lost to paperwork instead of patients, and whether AI could give some of it back.

It could. That project changed how I think about AI in medicine: not as a replacement for anything, but as a way to return time and attention to the people who need it most.

Featured project

AI-assisted clinical documentation workflow

Public health clinic (SUS) · Built with Claude

Designed and implemented an automation workflow for PEC (Prontuário Eletrônico do Cidadão), the record system used across Brazilian public clinics, to handle repetitive documentation the system doesn't streamline on its own.

2–3 min saved per 15-min visit ~25 daily consultations Self-initiated, end to end
